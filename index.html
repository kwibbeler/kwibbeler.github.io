Tri Plan Builder

An interactive triathlon training plan recommender. Pick a race distance, how far out you are, and how you like your week shaped, and it drafts a plan for you, from now through race week.

Shape your week

For each day, pick what you want to do, mark it "Any" to let the plan decide (including whether that day ends up as rest), or mark it "Rest" yourself. This same weekly shape carries through every week of your plan, from now to race week.

Built for Project 1 in CSCI 498E/598E, Coding with AI Agents. Not a substitute for a coach, and always leave room to adjust for how you actually feel.
